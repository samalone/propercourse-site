Proper Course Privacy

You’re invited to a race committee

Someone has invited you to help run races with Proper Course, an app that keeps a race course square to the wind and guides the mark boats to the right spots.

If Proper Course isn’t installed

Proper Course is in beta testing through Apple’s TestFlight and is not yet on the App Store. An early version for Android phones is being tried out too. Ask the person who invited you how to get the beta, or get in touch with us.

Once it’s installed, come back to the message with your invitation and tap the link again.

If Proper Course is installed

Tap the invitation link in Messages, Mail, or wherever you received it, rather than opening it in Safari. Your iPhone or iPad then opens it in Proper Course, which asks whether you want to join.

On an Android phone, tapping the link opens Proper Course the same way. If it opens this page in your browser instead, share the link to Proper Course from the browser’s Share menu. Or copy it, open Proper Course, tap Join…, and paste it.
